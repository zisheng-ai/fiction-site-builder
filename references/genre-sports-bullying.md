# Sports Team Bullying, Doping Frames, and Match Corruption

Use this reference for team exclusion, hazing, fabricated doping violations, abusive coaching, selection manipulation, match fixing, or athlete reputation destruction.

## Story Promise

The athlete's body is both her livelihood and the evidence used against her. She must protect health, eligibility, and team relationships while proving that performance systems were manipulated. Winning the final match cannot erase institutional abuse by itself.

## Conflict Engine

- Choose the competition structure, roster rules, testing workflow, medical authority, sponsorship pressure, and appeal path.
- Split opposition among coach, teammate beneficiary, medical or lab actor, federation gatekeeper, sponsor, and betting or ownership interest.
- Track training and injury consequences plausibly; effort is not immunity to physiology.
- Give teammates independent incentives to stay silent, defect, or expose the scheme.
- Make the protagonist decide whether competing helps truth or protects the system.

## Escalation Architecture

1. Open with public benching, a positive test, selection theft, or dangerous forced participation.
2. Preserve sample, schedule, equipment, or communication discrepancies.
3. An internal challenge worsens eligibility or team standing.
4. A teammate's action changes the case and the next competition.
5. Midpoint: the frame-up connects to match fixing, sponsorship, ownership, or a prior athlete.
6. The protagonist loses a key event but gains a witness or procedural opening.
7. Coordinate appeal evidence with a competition strategy that does not risk health.
8. Climax across both the public event and the institutional hearing.

## Character System

Use the targeted athlete, controlling coach, rival teammate, loyal teammate with competing ambition, team doctor or lab specialist, federation official, journalist or agent, and prior athlete harmed by the system.

## Public Reversal

The antagonist should rely on the scoreboard, test, or roster as unquestionable truth. The protagonist exposes how it was produced, then earns any athletic payoff under transparent conditions.

## Safety and Failure Modes

- Do not provide doping protocols or evasion instructions.
- Avoid treating injury, starvation, or overtraining as admirable discipline.
- Do not make every rival female athlete jealous or cruel.
- A romantic coach-athlete power imbalance should not be normalized.

## Quality Gate

The story passes when sport rules drive plot, physical cost remains real, teammates have agency, evidence challenges process rather than merely declaring innocence, and accountability extends past one game.
