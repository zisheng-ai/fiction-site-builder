# Japan-Market Character Visual Direction

Use this guide when a fiction site targets Japan (`ja-JP`) or when the brief supplies Japanese-market face references. It is a visual casting guide for covers and promotional images, not a claim that any character depicts a real person.

## Shared casting baseline

- Favor photorealistic Japanese adult characters with clear, natural skin texture, balanced facial proportions, expressive eyes, and believable styling. “Attractive” means memorable and emotionally legible at a small mobile thumbnail, not plastic perfection.
- Keep the cast visually distinct by silhouette, hair shape, expression, and styling. Do not give every character the same face, age, hairstyle, or makeup.
- Use supplied reference images as facial and styling anchors. Preserve the story's roles, action, wardrobe logic, and composition; do not paste a reference person's exact identity into a new scene.
- Avoid generic stock-photo faces, exaggerated cosmetic features, doll-like skin, anime rendering, heavy beauty filters, or age-inappropriate styling.

## Female lead

Target a refined, approachable Japanese beauty: bright almond-to-round eyes with a clear catchlight, soft brows, a small neat nose, naturally full lips, a gentle oval or softly tapered jaw, and long softly layered brown hair with face-framing strands. The expression should carry readable vulnerability and resolve (for example, tears with controlled composure), not a blank fashion pose. Makeup is light and polished: fresh skin, subtle eyeliner, natural blush, and a muted rose lip.

For cover thumbnails, keep her face unobstructed and large enough to read. Preserve a youthful adult impression without making her look like a teenager. Do not over-sharpen the eyes, over-enlarge them, or turn the face into a generic idol composite.

## Male lead

Target a youthful, elegant Japanese adult with strong “少年感” (youthful clarity) rather than an older-boss or middle-aged look: slim/lean facial structure, clean jawline, clear skin, straight dark brows, focused almond eyes, a neat straight nose, and naturally defined lips. Hair is medium-length black or very dark brown, softly layered and lightly tousled, with an airy side part or fringe falling naturally over the forehead. Wardrobe should read as contemporary high-status (for example, a well-cut dark suit) while the face remains fresh, restrained, and emotionally controlled.

The male lead should look composed and magnetic, never bulky, rugged, heavily stubbled, balding, or visibly middle-aged unless the brief explicitly asks for it. Keep facial beauty realistic and avoid copying a celebrity's exact likeness.

## Second male / rival or antagonist

Use a clearly separate but equally youthful Japanese adult face. The preferred reference direction is delicate and high-contrast: soft oval face, pale clear skin, large expressive dark eyes, straight brows, neat nose, soft lips, and medium-length tousled dark hair with airy fringe. He may read as more delicate, playful, or emotionally volatile than the lead, but must not become childlike, androgynous by accident, or a duplicate of the lead.

When the role is accusatory or shocked, preserve the reference face while adding an open-mouth reaction, lifted brows, and an active pointing or interrupting gesture. The expression must remain readable at thumbnail size; do not replace the emotional action with a neutral portrait.

## Acceptance checks

Before accepting a Japan-market cover, verify:

1. The female lead reads as polished, youthful adult, emotionally vulnerable, and recognizably distinct.
2. The male lead reads as youthful, elegant, clean-cut, and not an older “uncle” archetype.
3. The second male reads as a separate youthful face with the delicate fringe/eye direction when that reference is requested.
4. All three faces remain photorealistic, Japanese-market appropriate, and legible at mobile thumbnail scale.
5. The image preserves the required conflict tableau, role-specific action, and exact character count; visual refinement must not erase the story's hook.
