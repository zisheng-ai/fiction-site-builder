# Wrongful Conviction and Corrupt Justice

Use this reference for fabricated evidence, coerced confessions, scapegoating, wrongful imprisonment, corrupt police, witness intimidation, or a legal system protecting its prior verdict.

## Story Promise

The protagonist is trapped by a record that has already been declared true. The legal thriller earns satisfaction by showing how that record was produced, why institutions defend it, and what new fact can reopen the case. Innocence must become procedurally usable, not merely obvious to the reader.

## Conflict Engine

- Identify the original theory, evidence, witnesses, counsel failures, and finality barriers.
- Split opposition among the actual culprit, investigator, prosecutor or official, beneficiary, and institution protecting legitimacy.
- Give new evidence custody, admissibility, and interpretation problems.
- Let allies disagree over exoneration, plea deals, civil exposure, witness safety, and public campaigning.
- Use legal timelines as pressure without turning chapters into lectures.

## Escalation Architecture

1. Open with sentencing, parole denial, execution deadline, arrest, or discovery that the wrong person served time.
2. Find a discrepancy that attacks one link, not the whole case.
3. A witness recants or returns, creating credibility problems rather than certainty.
4. Evidence preservation fails or reveals official misconduct.
5. Midpoint: the frame-up protected a second crime, victim, or powerful beneficiary.
6. A legal ally risks license, office, or prior reputation.
7. Force the state to commit to a version under oath before introducing corroboration.
8. Resolve exoneration, accountability, and the aftermath of lost time separately.

## Character System

Use the accused or exoneration seeker, defense lawyer or investigator, original detective, prosecutor or institutional defender, victim's relative, recanting witness, journalist or advocate, and actual beneficiary of the false verdict.

## Evidence and Public Payoff

Build from independent sources: physical evidence, audit logs, alternate timeline, financial motive, witness corroboration, and official contradictions. The courtroom speech is only the delivery mechanism; the proof must already work.

## Safety and Failure Modes

- Do not treat incarceration trauma as a quick source of toughness.
- Avoid impossible surprise evidence with no disclosure or custody.
- Do not make every justice worker corrupt; institutional incentives and good-faith error can intensify conflict.
- Witness danger should be consequential, not disposable shock.

## Quality Gate

The story passes when the original conviction seems plausible, new evidence faces real barriers, supporting actors pursue conflicting remedies, and the ending distinguishes legal innocence, public belief, and personal repair.
