# Marital Control, Forced Marriage, and Contract Captivity

Use this reference for forced or strategic marriage, replacement brides, coercive contracts, wedding humiliation, financial confinement, or a spouse denied meaningful exit.

## Story Promise

Marriage is the visible instrument of control, not automatic romance. The protagonist must recover consent, mobility, property, and the right to define the relationship. A continuing romance is credible only after the coercive structure is dismantled and both adults can leave.

## Conflict Engine

- Specify the enforcement mechanism: debt, immigration status, custody, inheritance clause, family truce, medical proxy, reputation, or corporate vote.
- Distinguish the spouse from the architects. The spouse may enforce, resist, exploit, or gradually understand the arrangement, but cannot be absolved by attraction.
- Put exit terms on the page. Keys, accounts, documents, transport, counsel, and private communication must matter.
- Give the protagonist a goal beyond escaping the relationship, such as protecting a sibling, recovering an estate, or exposing the bargain.
- Treat any intimacy as consensual, adult, and separate from confinement.

## Escalation Architecture

1. Open at the signature, ceremony interruption, replacement reveal, or public refusal.
2. Establish one boundary the protagonist negotiates and one the system violates.
3. Reveal the marriage is covering a second transaction.
4. Transfer practical access: a key, account, vote, passport, or legal file.
5. Midpoint: the protagonist gains an exit but staying briefly serves a larger chosen goal.
6. The spouse must surrender a benefit or testify against their own side before reconciliation becomes possible.
7. Stage the public reversal at a wedding, family council, shareholder meeting, or court.
8. End with fresh terms created after the emergency, or a clean separation with consequences.

## Character System

Use both spouses, the bargain's architect, a rival beneficiary, an independent lawyer or registrar, a dependent whose welfare complicates exit, and a witness who once enabled the system. Supporting characters need independent goals and leverage.

## Public Reversal

Mirror the ceremony or contract language. Let the antagonist invoke a clause or vow, then reveal why it is void, fraudulent, superseded, or self-incriminating. Emotional declaration follows structural change, never substitutes for it.

## Safety and Failure Modes

- Never frame confinement, document seizure, intoxication, threats, or inability to consent as erotic proof.
- Do not let pregnancy function as punishment or a compulsory happy ending.
- Do not solve coercive control with the controlling spouse becoming more possessive toward others.
- Avoid contracts that ignore basic legal reality unless the story clearly identifies fraud or corruption.

## Quality Gate

The story passes when the protagonist makes consequential choices from Chapter 1, exit rights remain visible, romance does not erase accountability, and the ending leaves no coercive mechanism intact.
