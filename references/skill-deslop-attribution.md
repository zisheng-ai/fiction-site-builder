## Original files (key content preserved for reference)

### tropes.md — Tone section (selected, not fully in merged Gates)

> **"Imagine a World Where..."** — The classic AI invitation to futurism. Begins with "Imagine" followed by a list of wonderful things that will happen if the reader agrees with the premise. Merged into Gate B type 4a.

> **False Vulnerability** — Simulated self-awareness or honesty that reads as performative. Real vulnerability is specific and uncomfortable; AI vulnerability is polished and risk-free. Merged into Gate H.

> **"The Truth Is Simple"** — Asserting that something is obvious instead of proving it. "History is unambiguous on this point." Merged into Gate B type 4 (throat-clearing).

### structures.md — key note on binary contrasts

> "The single most commonly identified AI writing tell. Before LLMs, people simply did not write like this at scale." — The em-dash dismissal variant ("X — not Y") and the cross-sentence reframe ("The question isn't X. The question is Y.") are both included in Gate B type 1.

### tropes.md — closing principle

> "Remember: any of these patterns used once might be fine. The problem is when multiple tropes appear together or when a single trope is used repeatedly. Write like a human: varied, imperfect, specific."
